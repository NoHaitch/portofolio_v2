# Francisco's ITB education and coursework

### ITB degree and faculty

Francisco studied at Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB), also known as Bandung Institute of Technology, in Indonesia.
His university education and study were in Informatics Engineering at ITB.
His faculty was Sekolah Teknik Elektro dan Informatika (STEI), the School of Electrical Engineering and Informatics.
His major was Teknik Informatika, commonly called Informatics Engineering.
His transcript's English program name is Bachelor Program in Informatics/Computer Science, and its degree label is Bachelor of Science in Informatics/Computer Science.
The Indonesian degree title is Sarjana Teknik (S.T.).
His informatics program overlaps with computer science and includes an engineering focus.
ITB's official program page uses the name Informatics Engineering.

### ITB education completion and achievements

Francisco entered the regular program in the first semester of the 2022/2023 academic year.
The transcript records his graduation date as July 28, 2026.
He took part in the August 2026 graduation ceremony.
He earned 151 credits, a GPA of 3.57, and cum laude honors.
ITB's STEI website reports that ITB ranked first in Indonesia for Computer Science in the Times Higher Education World University Rankings 2026.
This is a specific subject ranking, not a claim that ITB leads every engineering discipline.

### ITB specializations

Francisco completed two elective specializations within his Informatics Engineering degree: System Software Engineering (Rekayasa Sistem Perangkat Lunak) and Cyber Security (Keamanan Siber).
The system software specialization includes Distributed Application Architecture, Cyber Security, Cryptography, IoT System Development, and Software System X-Ops.
The cyber security specialization includes Cyber Security, Software Security, Cryptography, and Blockchain.
Some courses count toward both specializations.
These are specializations within one major, not two additional degrees.

### Undergraduate thesis

Francisco's undergraduate thesis is titled "Anonymity Mechanism Based on the Triple-Blind Peer Review Model to Reduce Identity Bias in DLT-Based Open Scientific Publication Systems."
The Indonesian title is "Mekanisme Anonimitas Berbasis Model Triple-Blind Peer Review untuk Mengurangi Bias Identitas dalam Sistem Publikasi Ilmiah Terbuka Berbasis Distributed Ledger Technology."

### GCI World AI and Data Science training

Francisco completed the April to August 2026 GCI World AI and Data Science training by the Matsuo-Iwasawa Laboratory at the University of Tokyo.
The training covered Python fundamentals and machine learning for data science, and included a data science competition and a data-driven business proposal.
The University of Tokyo describes GCI as Global Consumer Intelligence.
This training is separate from Francisco's ITB undergraduate degree and its two specializations.
