On July 8, 2026, I defended my undergraduate thesis at Institut Teknologi Bandung.
It marked the end of a full year of work, difficult deadlines, and more than a few burnouts.

## Thesis

**Indonesian:** _Mekanisme Anonimitas Berbasis Model Triple-Blind Peer Review Untuk Mengurangi Bias Identitas Dalam Sistem Publikasi Ilmiah Terbuka Berbasis Distributed Ledger Technology_

**English:** _Anonymity Mechanism Based on the Triple-Blind Peer Review Model to Reduce Identity Bias in DLT-Based Open Scientific Publication Systems_

My work explored how a triple-blind peer review model could help reduce identity bias in an open scientific publishing system built on distributed ledger technology.
I worked through the problem, analysis, and resulting design carefully, aiming to make the reasoning clear from the initial problem through to the final result.
There were still gaps, but I was satisfied with what I had built, and I was grateful that the work was appreciated.

## A long and difficult year

The thesis took a full year of work.
At several points, I was so focused on meeting deadlines and making the result as complete as I could that I fell into a two-day sleep cycle.
Looking back, I can see how perfectionistic I became.
It was exhausting and led to burnout, but that same attention to detail also pushed me to make the work coherent and carefully reasoned.

## Waiting for the result

Before the defence, I was more nervous than I had ever been.
After presenting, I had to wait outside the room while the examiners discussed the result.
Those minutes felt especially long.
